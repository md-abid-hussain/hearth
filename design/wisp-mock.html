Design direction

Two surfaces. Wisp lives small,
the dashboard keeps the record.

The companion is a floating pill that sits on top of your work, like a dictation widget. Everything else, the reports, the reasons Wisp stayed quiet, connectors and settings, lives in the dashboard window you open when you want it.

3. Connectors

Same window, Setup group. The read-only gate is the story here.

What Wisp will never do

  • Send, reply to or draft an email
  • Create, edit or delete a calendar event
  • Run a tool whose server marks it destructive

What it can read when you ask

  • Your next three meetings, to time a break around them
  • Unread email subjects, never the bodies unless you ask
  • Notes in a folder you chose
